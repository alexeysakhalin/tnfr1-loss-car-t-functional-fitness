## Single-cell interpretation boundary

The tumor-co-culture analysis yields eleven computational clusters. C10 is
retained in complete QC outputs but excluded from the transferred C0–C9
signature family. This manual exclusion and its marker evidence must be
reported with the clean single-cell rerun; cell-level marker-test p-values are
descriptive and are not biological-replicate inference. Cluster proportions
from computationally retained cells are not absolute live-cell phenotype
frequencies.
